Grid Infect – Privacy Policy

Effective 17 September 2026. Applies to the Grid Infect app published by Bloodhound Studios (com.bloodhoundstudios.gridinfect.app) on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Summary

Grid Infect has no accounts and no servers of its own, and contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Bloodhound Studios itself never receives any personal information about you.

The app is not, however, data-free. It shows ads through Google AdMob, and to serve and measure those ads the AdMob SDK collects the following from your device and shares it with Google and Google's advertising partners for advertising purposes:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location;
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, app version, language, screen size);
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped);
  • your consent state — the choices you made in the consent form.

Every one of those five categories is both collected and shared, and the purpose is advertising. None of it is sold for money. None of it reaches Bloodhound Studios. Where consent is required, ads are non-personalised until you give it. The one in-app purchase (remove ads) is handled by the app store you installed from. Nothing else leaves your device.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, results, the Lock wallet, and your settings (language, sound) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. You can erase progress and results from the in-app Settings screen, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Interstitial and rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location;
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, app version, language, screen size);
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped);
  • your consent state — the choices you made in the consent form.

This data goes to Google, not to Bloodhound Studios. How Google uses it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, and opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's settings, and manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Ads are not personalised for users who have not consented where consent is required.

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law, the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. The game is fully playable whatever you choose. You can review or change your choices at any time from Settings ▸ Privacy options inside the app.

United States state privacy rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and under comparable laws in other US states, serving personalised ads through AdMob counts as sharing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. That description applies to Grid Infect. Bloodhound Studios does not sell personal information for money and shares it for no purpose other than advertising, but the sharing does happen whenever personalised ads are served.

To stop it, open Settings ▸ Privacy options inside the app and decline personalised ads. The choice is passed to the AdMob SDK and applies from the next ad request. You can also switch ad personalisation off at device level: on Android, Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads lets you delete the advertising ID or opt out of personalisation; on iOS, Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking lets you deny Grid Infect permission to track, which withholds the IDFA. Either route ends the sharing described above. The game stays fully playable, and non-personalised ads continue unless you buy the remove-ads purchase.

Grid Infect collects no sensitive personal information and makes no automated decisions about you. There is no account to close and no profile for us to delete, because we hold neither. To reach data already held by Google, use the Google links in the Advertising section above.

In-app purchase

Grid Infect offers a single one-time purchase that removes interstitial ads. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; Bloodhound Studios never receives your name, payment method or billing details. The app reads the store's own purchase record to know whether you own the purchase, and that record stays with the store under its terms.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are included.
  • No account, login, or contact details are requested.
  • No data is shared with anyone other than the ad and store services named above.

Children

Grid Infect is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region) and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because Bloodhound Studios holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on request. Rights over data processed by Google or your app store are exercised with them, through the links above and your device or store account settings.
