Grid Infect – Age Suitability

Effective 17 September 2026. Applies to the Grid Infect app published by Bloodhound Studios (com.bloodhoundstudios.gridinfect.app).

Rating

Grid Infect carries an Apple calculated age rating of 4+ and contains no objectionable content. There is no violence, horror, sexual content, nudity, profanity, gambling or simulated gambling, and no reference to alcohol, tobacco or drugs. There is no in-game currency, no loot box, and no randomised reward that can be bought. It is an abstract grid puzzle.

Not directed at children

Grid Infect is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). It is not in Apple's Kids Category and Apple's "Made for Kids" designation is Not Applicable. It is not enrolled in Google Play's Families programme, and its declared Play target audience is 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Advertising and purchases

The app contains third-party advertising served by Google AdMob: interstitial ads, and rewarded ads the player chooses to watch. It contains one optional in-app purchase, a single one-time purchase that removes interstitial ads.

Ads are non-personalised wherever consent is absent or is required and has not been given. In regions where a consent form is required, one from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before any ad is requested, and the choice can be changed at any time from Settings ▸ Privacy options. What the ad SDK collects and shares is set out in the privacy policy; this page does not restate it.

What the app does not contain

No user-generated content. No chat, messaging or comments. No social features, no friend lists, no online leaderboards, no accounts and no sign-in. Gameplay contains no links out of the app: no web links, no store links, no social media. The only thing that can open a destination outside the app is an advertisement, and only if the player taps it.
